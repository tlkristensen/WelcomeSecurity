# Security Policy
 
WelcomeSecurity takes security seriously. We appreciate the security research community
and welcome responsible disclosure of vulnerabilities.
  
## Reporting Guidelines
 
1. Email security reports to info@welcomesecurity.net
2. Include the affected asset, steps to reproduce, and the security impact
3. Allow reasonable time for us to respond and fix issues before any public disclosure
4. Do not access or modify user data
5. Do not perform denial of service attacks
 
## Rewards
 
This is a disclosure-only policy. WelcomeSecurity does not offer monetary rewards
for vulnerability reports. With your permission, we are happy to credit researchers
publicly for valid findings.
 
## Response Timeline
 
- We will acknowledge receipt within 24 hours
- Initial assessment within 72 hours
- Resolution timeline depends on severity and complexity
 
Thank you for helping keep WelcomeSecurity and our clients secure!
